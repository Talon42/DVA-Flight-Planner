## UI

- Some airlines have been given dark mode specific logos to improve visibility

## Tours/Accomplishments

- Submitted PIREPs now remove required flights from Tours/Accomplishment eligibility when a fresh Sync is performed. If a PIREP is rejected, the flight will be added back to the required list.

## Functionality

- Users will now be unable to add flights to their flight board if the schedule is out of date. This is to prevent generating PIREPs for expired flights accidentally. Users will be required to Sync their schedule to continue.
