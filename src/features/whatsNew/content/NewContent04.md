Open Logbook > Pilot Stats for a snapshot of your flying career. Review total legs, distance, block time, flight time, and average landing rate, then explore your most-flown airlines and aircraft, recent landings, and top airports. Use View all for the full breakdowns, switch dashboard pages for more highlights, or choose a period from the filter to focus your stats.
