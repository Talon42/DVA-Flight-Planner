Add extra preparation time to your SimBrief departure. In Settings > SimBrief, choose a Departure Offset of 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes. The offset is added to your selected dispatch time source, whether you use Schedule UTC or Current UTC. Choose None to use the original time.
