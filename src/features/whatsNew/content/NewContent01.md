Airline Overrides customize your next dispatch. Historic Flights can use schedule codes such as DVH and KLH or real airline codes such as DAL and KLM. For Delta Connection flights, choose Default (DLC), Delta (DAL), Endeavor (EDV), Skywest (SKW), Republic (RPA), or Random to pick a regional carrier each time you generate a plan. Your choices save automatically and apply to the airline prefix and callsign when generating or regenerating a SimBrief plan.
