Regenerate your Simbrief flight plans directly with the ability to choose a new aircraft. This was also update your current DVA draft flight report. Use Regenerate if you want a brand new Simbrief plan from scratch. Use Refresh if you modified the flight plan directly on the Simbrief website and want the changes updated on the app.
